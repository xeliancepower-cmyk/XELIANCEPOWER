Contest

About The Contest — Environmental Stewardship Challenge

Contest Overview

Xeliance Power is organizing a promotional contest where participants have the opportunity to win a 3 BHK residential flat measuring 1365 sq. ft., located at a prime location on Main Sikar Road, Jaipur, valued at approximately ₹80,00,000.

The contest objective is to favor and support the environment by sourcing innovative, actionable, and sustainable strategies to combat critical environmental issues, such as severe urban air pollution.

This is not a random selection or lottery; selection will be based strictly on the originality, feasibility, and measurable impact of the proposed solution.

Participation Fees & Packages

Participants can enter the contest through one of the three available package tiers:

  • Basic Entry (₹1,200): Grants a single entry slot and full eligibility to win any of the contest prizes.
  • Standard Entry (₹2,000): Grants a single entry slot, full eligibility to win any of the contest prizes, plus guaranteed cashback equivalent benefits.
  • Premium Entry (₹3,000): Grants a single entry slot, full eligibility to win any of the contest prizes, plus complete professional house drawings and architectural PDF plans.
  • All fees are one-time payments and are completely non-refundable under any circumstances.

Eligibility

  • Open to Indian residents aged 18 years and above.
  • Employees, partners, and immediate family members of Xeliance Power are not eligible.
  • Submissions must be entirely original work. Plagiarism will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Submissions must directly address a defined environmental problem and propose a concrete intervention.

Prizes & Rewards

A total of 100 winners will be selected based on evaluation. The prize distribution is structured as follows:

  • 1st Prize (1 Winner): Exclusive ownership of a Luxury 3BHK Flat in Jaipur (1365 sq. ft.).
  • 2nd Prize (1 Winner): Exclusive ownership of a BMW Motorcycle.
  • 3rd Prize (1 Winner): Premium Mystery Box worth up to ₹2 Lakh, including luxury electronics (like the latest iPhone, Dyson Hair Dryer) and makeup/skincare products from d'Alba, MAC, Laneige, and Beauty of Joseon.
  • 4th to 10th Prize (7 Winners): ₹51,000 Cash Prize each.
  • 11th to 100th Prize (90 Winners): ₹10,000 Cash Prize each.

Grand Prize — Flat Details

  • One participant will win the 3 BHK flat (1365 sq ft) at Main Sikar Road, Jaipur.
  • Registration, stamp duty, and any legally required charges, if applicable, must be borne by the winner.
  • The winning submission, as determined by the Jury Panel, will secure the Grand Prize.

No Additional Charges

Apart from the selected participation package fee (₹1,200, ₹2,000, or ₹3,000), no additional charges will be demanded for entering, participating, or claiming the rewards.

Winner Announcement

All winners, including the grand prize winner and the Top 100 assured prize winners, will be announced on the official website only.

Judging & Review Process

  • All submitted entries will undergo a rigorous, non-random evaluation process.
  • The Jury Panel will review submissions based on: Originality, Feasibility, Measurable Impact, and Scalability.
  • The decision of the Jury Panel is final and binding in all respects.
