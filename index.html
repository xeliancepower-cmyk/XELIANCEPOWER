Why Finland?

Finland has been ranked the world’s happiest country for years running. Quiet, honest and beautiful, it’s a place where people actually talk to each other.

🌌

Northern lights

Lapland offers some of the best aurora viewing in Europe, from roughly late August to April.

🧖

Sauna culture

There are millions of saunas for a population of about 5.5 million. Conversation flows when the steam does.

🦌

Lapland adventures

Husky rides, reindeer farms, glass igloos and snowshoe walks under the stars.

🏙️

Helsinki

Design cafés, waterfront markets, island ferries and a lively food and music scene.

🌲

Lakes and forests

Around 180,000 lakes, midnight sun in summer and cosy cabins all year.

☕

Coffee and calm

Finns drink more coffee per person than anyone. Slow down and share a cup.

FAQ

When is the best time to visit?

For northern lights, September to March. For midnight sun and lake life, June to August.

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your nationality. Finland is in the Schengen Area, so check entry rules for your passport before travelling.

Is the trip guaranteed to lead to love?

No one can promise that! We promise great experiences, good company and a story worth telling.
